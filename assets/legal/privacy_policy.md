# GoBuddy Privacy Policy

**Effective date: August 19, 2026**

This Privacy Policy explains how GoBuddy handles information when you use the mobile application.

## 1. Information we may process

Depending on the features you use, GoBuddy may process:

- account details, such as your name, email address, profile image, and authentication identifiers;
- profile and matchmaking preferences you choose to provide;
- group, trip, collaboration, and safety-planning information;
- expense, budget, participant, receipt, balance, and settlement records;
- app settings, language, appearance, and subscription status;
- device, diagnostic, crash, and usage information; and
- photos, files, or other content you choose to upload.

## 2. How information is used

Information may be used to:

- create and secure your account;
- provide matchmaking, collaboration, safety, and expense features;
- synchronize information and show it to group members you select;
- maintain, troubleshoot, and improve GoBuddy;
- detect abuse, fraud, and security incidents; and
- comply with applicable legal obligations.

## 3. Device storage and service providers

Some information may remain locally on your device. Features that require authentication, synchronization, analytics, crash reporting, purchases, or remote content may send relevant information to service providers used by GoBuddy.

These providers process information under their own terms and privacy practices. GoBuddy does not sell your personal information.

## 4. Sharing

Information may be shared:

- with users or groups you intentionally interact with;
- with service providers that operate app functionality;
- when required by law or a valid legal process; or
- when necessary to protect users, rights, safety, and service integrity.

Do not add sensitive information to shared groups or expense records unless the intended participants should have access to it.

## 5. Retention and deletion

Information is retained only as reasonably needed for the purposes described above, subject to technical, contractual, and legal requirements. You may stop using the app or request account deletion through available account controls. Some records may remain temporarily in backups or where retention is legally required.

## 6. Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational safeguards, but no storage or transmission method is completely secure. Protect your device and account credentials, and notify us if you suspect unauthorized access.

## 7. Your choices and rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, restrict, or obtain a copy of personal information. You can also control certain permissions through your device settings. Disabling a required permission may limit the related feature.

## 8. Children's privacy

GoBuddy is not intended for children who cannot legally consent to data processing in their jurisdiction. If you believe a child has provided personal information without appropriate permission, contact us.

## 9. International processing

Service providers may process information in countries other than your own. Applicable safeguards will be used where required by law.

## 10. Policy changes

We may update this policy as GoBuddy evolves. The effective date above identifies the latest version. Material changes may be communicated in the app.
